ESP32 smart alarm clock and custom PCB

Personal project, Sep. 2026 to present. Solo build.

Designed a custom 2-layer PCB in KiCad that combines an ESP32, OLED display, RTC, ambient-light sensor, buzzer driver, user-input buttons, and USB-C power.

Took the product from requirements and breadboard prototyping through schematic, layout, assembly, firmware, and validation. Components were chosen by reading datasheets and checking electrical and package constraints.

Firmware in C/C++ handles adjustable alarms, time display, menu navigation, snooze, and peripheral control.

Course project, Jun. to Aug. 2026.

Designed and assembled custom PCBs in KiCad for IR sensing, metal detection, and embedded control, including soldering and hardware bring-up.
